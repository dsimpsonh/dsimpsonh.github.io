Ventures and projects

What came before.

2014 – 2017 · Co-founder and Chief Product Officer

Shake Your Power CIC

SPARK, the first energy-generating musical instrument. Granted a US patent and two London Design Award Golds (SPARK in 2014, DIY SPARK in 2015). STEM pilots in the UK, the US and India, with UCL Academy and Pratham; manufactured in Shenzhen.

2014 – · Founder

Monogram Publishers

Independent publishing house. Publisher of The Art of Getting Stuff Done (2022).

monogrampublishers.com →
2012 – 2014 · Founder and designer

Glass Lab / Trash Surface Bureau

Recycled-glass surface products made from the commercial waste of London businesses. Featured in FRAME Magazine, Inhabitat and Designer Pages.

2012 · Royal College of Art

Fictional Objects

Speculative objects for futures that do not exist yet (the Human Behaviour Control Machine, the Lightning Harvester, the Prayer Transmitter, the Dream Time Machine), made alongside the MA dissertation on myth and design.
