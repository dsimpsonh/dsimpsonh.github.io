The methods

Eight methods, one discipline.

Data has no value on its own. Decisions do. Each method starts from a specific decision, question, venture or team and makes it measurably better.

LEVER · The unit is a decision

Align the goal. Expose the friction. Sequence the fix.

Decision diagnostics for organisations that have adopted AI and cannot show what it changed. LEVER maps every recurring decision, prices the friction in each, and sequences the one change that recovers it without breaking something downstream.

Read the method →
RECALL · The unit is a question

Ask first. Score the corpus. Earn the right to build.

A retrieval system cannot be better than the documents it retrieves from. RECALL measures a company's corpus against the questions people actually ask before anything is built, and can recommend against building.

Read the method →
STAKE · The unit is a revenue stream

Build it. Run it. Let the partner sell it.

A product-partnership model for businesses that own a vertical and need a product they cannot build themselves, with a revenue ledger per deal.

Read the method →
FLYWHEEL · The unit is a venture

Score the business on eight dimensions. Find the drag.

A venture diagnostic used with founders and students: eight dimensions, scored from evidence, to find which one is slowing everything else.

Read the method →
Vibecoding · The unit is a shipped product

From a worthy problem to a live URL.

The twenty-step protocol taught at IED Madrid, LCC and in company training: how people who are not engineers ship working software with AI coding agents, under specification and review gates.

Read the method →
REWIRED · The unit is the operator

Build the operator before you build the company.

A diagnostic and protocol for the founder's own working defaults: the habits and beliefs that decide what gets built.

Read the method →
TeamScale · The unit is a team

Build an AI-native team. Don't over-hire.

Roles, benchmarks and a twelve-month roadmap for teams designed around AI from the start.

Read the method →

Every insight carries its trail from grounds to claim.
