A brief is a fit if
  • The outcome is a decision that gets measurably better
  • There is data with recorded outcomes, or a corpus someone needs answers from
  • A named sponsor owns the result
  • 2–4 days a week, 2–6 months, outside IR35
It is not a fit if
  • The role is pure ML engineering with no decision owner
  • The deliverable is a slide deck about AI, with nothing to ship
  • It's inside IR35, five days a week, or open-ended
  • It's in property or wealth management
First thirty days

The method, applied in-house.

Week 1 — Goal Ladder. One goal, in the board's words, with its number attached. Signed before anything is mapped.

Weeks 2–3 — Blueprint and Ledger. Every recurring decision, who owns it, what's recorded, and a Friction Index scored from observation, not interviews.

Week 4 — Sequenced plan. The shortest safe order of changes, priced with the Leverage Equation, with the things I'd leave alone named as clearly as the things I'd build.

It's LEVER and RECALL, run from the inside. Read them first; that's what they're for.
