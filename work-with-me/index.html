Guarantees

What every engagement holds to.

  • Engine-neutral. Standard open-source tooling only. None of Aletheai's core, none of its behavioural primitives, in any client build.
  • Honest gates. LQ < 3, AQ < 3, no gold set, no outcome labels — each one stops the build and says so in writing.
